# SYN-TC-4310 Superseded Retirement Contribution Credit Provision and Replacement

This document is synthetic training content from the fictional Northstar Synthetic Tax Planning Authority. All rates, thresholds, dates, and limits in this document are invented and must not be treated as real tax law.

## SYN-TC-4310-a Superseded retirement credit provision

Effective 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31. This superseded synthetic provision allowed a maximum retirement contribution credit of 1,100 for starter households and 1,900 for established households.

Advisors may cite this section only for historical training scenarios dated before 2026-01-01. It must not be used for current-year recommendations once the replacement provision applies.

This section exists to test date-sensitive retrieval. Its topic and language are intentionally close to the replacement provision, but the effective period and credit amounts are different. A scenario date before 2026-01-01 should retrieve this section when the question asks for the old rule.

### SYN-TC-4310-c Superseded provision warning

Any explanation using this section must say that the provision is superseded. The citation should include the effective-through date so downstream retrieval can distinguish it from the replacement.

If a retrieved explanation omits the effective-through date, the reviewer should request a correction because the reader may confuse old and current synthetic treatment.

## SYN-TC-4310-b Replacement retirement credit provision

Effective 2026-01-01. This replacement synthetic provision allows a maximum retirement contribution credit of 1,600 for starter households and 2,400 for established households.

Advisors should cite this section for current synthetic retirement-credit planning after the effective date. The replacement updates the invented credit amounts but does not change the phase-out range described in SYN-TC-4300-a.

This section should be paired with SYN-TC-4300-a when a recommendation needs both the maximum credit amount and the income phase-out range. It should be paired with SYN-PB-04-c when the workflow issue is correcting a superseded citation.

### SYN-TC-4310-d Replacement effective-date note

When both superseded and replacement sections appear in retrieval results, the recommendation date controls which section should be cited.

The current recommendation date should be recorded in the citation note. If no date is present, the advisor should ask for clarification rather than guessing which synthetic provision applies.
