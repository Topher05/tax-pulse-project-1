# SYN-PB-02 Playbook for Deduction Bunching and Cap Review

This document is synthetic training content from the fictional Northstar Synthetic Tax Planning Authority. All rates, thresholds, dates, and limits in this document are invented and must not be treated as real tax law.

## SYN-PB-02-a Deduction bunching triage

Begin with the client's projected itemized deductions and compare them to the relevant synthetic cap provision. If the projection is materially below the cap, bunching may not be worth reviewer time. If the projection is near or above the cap, document the timing option.

The advisor should identify the planning year before selecting a cap citation. The 2026 and 2027 rule provisions are deliberately similar, so year discipline is part of the retrieval training.

### SYN-PB-02-b Advisor review thresholds for deduction bunching

Use this invented table to decide whether to write a recommendation note.

| review signal | invented threshold amount | advisor action |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| projected deductions below cap | 5000 | monitor only |
| projected deductions near cap | 2500 | draft timing note |
| projected deductions above cap | 1 | request reviewer approval |

These thresholds are workflow thresholds, not rule thresholds. They help the advisor decide when to write, revise, or escalate a recommendation. The legal-style source for the cap remains SYN-TC-4200-a or SYN-TC-4200-b.

## SYN-PB-02-c Cap citation requirement

When the recommendation depends on a cap, cite SYN-TC-4200-a for 2026 or SYN-TC-4200-b for 2027. The playbook citation explains the workflow, while the rule citation explains the synthetic threshold.

If the client explanation mentions charitable contributions, add a contribution-limit citation only when the percentage limit affects the recommendation. Do not add unrelated citations merely because they share the deduction topic.

### SYN-PB-02-d Client-facing caution

Client-facing summaries should say that the numbers are planning assumptions from synthetic training content. They should not describe invented caps as statutory caps.

The summary should preserve enough detail for review: planning year, projected deduction amount, cap citation, and recommended timing action.
