# SYN-TC-4120 Quarterly Underpayment Exception Rules

This document is synthetic training content from the fictional Northstar Synthetic Tax Planning Authority. All rates, thresholds, dates, and limits in this document are invented and must not be treated as real tax law.

## SYN-TC-4120-a Seasonal income exception

A household with seasonal synthetic income may use a quarterly exception review when at least 70 percent of the annual non-withheld income is expected in one quarter. The advisor must identify the quarter, the income source, and the payment date used for planning.

This exception does not waive payment analysis. It only permits the advisor to compare payment timing against the season in which the income is expected to arrive.

The exception should be used when the timing of income is the central planning fact. It should not be retrieved first for a general query about dividends, consulting receipts, or non-withheld income unless the query also indicates that income arrives unevenly across quarters.

### SYN-TC-4120-b Documentation for seasonal exception

The planning note must include the projected income by quarter and the reason the income is seasonal. Acceptable synthetic reasons include annual bonus concentration, consulting project completion, business asset sale, or one-time dividend event.

The advisor should keep the quarterly projection table in the case workpapers, not inside the citation text. The citation should identify this section and summarize the timing reason in one sentence.

## SYN-TC-4120-c De minimis underpayment exception

An underpayment is treated as de minimis for training purposes when the projected shortfall is less than 600 and less than 3 percent of the total projected payment requirement. These amounts are invented limits.

This rule differs from the safe-harbor provisions because it begins with a projected shortfall rather than a projected payment amount. Retrieval should prefer this section when a recommendation asks whether a small remaining gap can be documented rather than paid.

### SYN-TC-4120-d Exception conflict rule

If the seasonal income exception and de minimis exception both apply, the advisor must cite the rule that best explains the recommendation. The citation should not combine the rules into one blended authority.

When the recommendation turns on both timing and amount, the reviewer should request two citations. The rule citation explains the exception; the playbook citation can explain the advisor workflow.
